# BUG-033 — Ethics landing offered 7 PYQ years, only 1 has questions; 62 seeded practice questions were unreachable

**Status:** PARTIALLY FIXED | **Severity:** MEDIUM | **Session:** S47 | **Category:** content-gap + navigation

## Symptom
1. `/upsc/ethics` "Solved Past Years" tab showed year cards for 2019–2025. Clicking any year except 2025 → "No questions found for this paper."
2. `/upsc/ethics` "Practice Papers" tab showed "No practice papers available yet" despite 62 practice questions (`ethics_prac_1`, `ethics_prac_2`, 31 each) existing in `ethics_questions`.
3. "Pattern Synthesis" tab showed "No concept/scenario data available" — `ethics_concept_analysis` / `ethics_scenario_analysis` tables were created (m044) but never seeded by any migration.

## Root Cause
1. `ethics_paper_bp.py` had `_ETHICS_YEARS = list(range(2019, 2026))` hardcoded, independent of actual data. Only `ethics_pyq_2025` (31 questions) was ever seeded by m052.
2. `ethics_landing()` builds its Practice Papers list from the `ethics_practice_papers` metadata table, not from `ethics_questions` directly. m052 seeded question rows under `paper_id='ethics_prac_1'/'ethics_prac_2'` but never inserted the corresponding `ethics_practice_papers` rows — content existed, no route to reach it.
3. No migration ever populated `ethics_concept_analysis` / `ethics_scenario_analysis` — genuinely empty, not a bug, just undone work.

## Fix
- `ethics_paper_bp.py::_get_pyq_years()` now derives years from `SELECT DISTINCT paper_year FROM ethics_questions WHERE content_type='pyq'` instead of a hardcoded range — only years with real content are offered.
- `migrations/m056_register_ethics_practice_papers.py` registers `ethics_prac_1`/`ethics_prac_2` in `ethics_practice_papers`, making the existing 62 questions reachable.

## Not fixed (content gap, needs a generation session)
- Only 1 of 7 real PYQ years (2019–2025) has ethics questions. 2019–2024 have zero.
- `ethics_concept_analysis` / `ethics_scenario_analysis` are empty — Pattern Synthesis tab will stay empty until seeded.
