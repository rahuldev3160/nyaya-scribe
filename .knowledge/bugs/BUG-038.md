# BUG-038 — Eco-Optional PYQ bank ~48% incomplete for 2021-2025 (both papers)

**Status:** FIXED (2026-09-17, S52) | **Severity:** HIGH | **Session:** S52 | **Category:** content-gap

## Background
A same-day read-only audit found `data/upsc_eco_opt.db`'s `pyq_questions` table missing
roughly half of every checkable UPSC Economics Optional year (2021-2025), for both Paper 1
and Paper 2, against the real exam structure verified from actual scanned official booklets:
**28 lettered sub-parts per paper per year** — Q1 and Q5 compulsory with 5 sub-parts each
(a-e), Q2/Q3/Q4/Q6/Q7/Q8 with 3 sub-parts each (a/b/c).

## Method
Per Rahul's verify-before-building rule, did not trust the audit's summary numbers blindly.
OCR'd all 10 real source PDFs (genuine scanned official booklets, image-only, no text layer)
at `~/Desktop/UPSC/Mains/Optional/Economics/PYQs_paper{1,2}/{2021..2025}.pdf` via
`pdftoppm -r 400 -png` → `tesseract --psm 6` (both already installed locally). English-half
OCR quality was excellent on every page (the parallel Hindi text, never used, OCR'd poorly —
irrelevant to this task). For each of the 10 paper-years, independently transcribed the full
Q1-Q8 structure (all lettered sub-parts) and cross-matched **by question content**, not just
row count, against the DB's existing rows to derive the exact missing list.

All 10 independently-derived missing counts matched the audit's per-year summary numbers
(24/28, 6/28, 12/28, 16/28, 6/28 for P1 2021-2025; 24/28, 15/28, 22/28, 17/28, 4/28 for P2
2021-2025). The two years where the audit had itemized the exact missing sub-parts (P1 2024,
P2 2024) matched this session's independent re-derivation exactly, letter-for-letter.

## Real surprises found in the process
1. **P2 2022 Q7(a)** ("Critically examine the various poverty alleviation programmes in India
   since 1970's") had no clean year=2022 row of its own — but the exact text was already
   present in the DB, concatenated onto the front of an unrelated year=2021 row
   (`upsc_p2_0137`), a parsing leftover from the original ecoholics ingestion (a stray
   `(2022)` marker sits mid-string in that row, which is what gave away the source of the
   bleed). `upsc_p2_0137` was left untouched (pre-existing, out of scope); a clean new
   year=2022 row was added for the same real text instead.
2. The row-granularity inconsistency flagged by the audit (some multi-part answers split
   across 2 rows) is real and reproduced exactly once above (P1 2025 Q4(b)) — confirmed
   directly rather than assumed.
