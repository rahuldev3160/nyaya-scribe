STAKE Framework
Section B — Case studies with 3 sub-parts (a/b/c)
Step Label What to write Sub-part Word target
S Stakeholders List all parties; their interests and rights at stake Part (a) 60–80w
T Tension Name the core ethical conflict; opposing duties/values Part (a) 40–60w
A Analysis Apply ethical frameworks (utilitarian/deontological/virtue); weigh options Part (b) 80–100w
K Key Decision State your chosen course of action; justify with reasoning Part (b) 50–70w
E Execution Practical steps as an officer; mitigations; systemic recommendation Part (c) 80–100w

{# Worked example #}
Worked example sketch
S Stakeholders: the officer, subordinate staff, the contractor, local community affected by the project, and taxpayers.
T Tension: duty of loyalty to superior vs. duty of integrity and public accountability.
A Analysis: deontological duty to report overrides consequentialist fear of career harm; virtue ethics demands courage.
K Key decision: file a vigilance report, exhaust internal channels first, preserve documentary evidence.
E Execution: set up an independent technical audit, recommend whistleblower protection policy, and flag systemic procurement gap.
