Essay + Précis + Reading Comprehension, one 90-minute clock. This trains completion under time pressure — not content quality (that's already scored elsewhere).

How this works
  • Three sections, one after another: Essay → Précis → Reading Comprehension.
  • Each section gets a {{ budget_min }}-minute budget (an even split — RBI's real per-section mark split isn't officially published).
  • Running over budget shows a warning, but never force-cuts you into the next section.
  • Live words/min is tracked while you type. At the end you get a pacing report showing exactly where the time went.
